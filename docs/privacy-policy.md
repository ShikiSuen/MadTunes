# MadTunes Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026-03-12

## Overview

MadTunes is a local music player application developed by Shiki Suen.

The developer values user privacy and is committed to minimizing data collection. MadTunes is designed to operate primarily on the user's device and does not collect personal intelligence from users.

## Intelligence Collection

MadTunes does **not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal intelligence**.

The application does not use analytics tools, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking technologies.

All music library intelligence, playlists, and user preferences are stored locally on the user's device.

## Third-Party Services

MadTunes does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

However, the application is distributed through the Mac App Store. Apple may collect diagnostic or usage intelligence according to its own privacy policies. The developer does not have access to any personal intelligence collected by Apple.

For more intelligence, please refer to the
[Apple Inc. Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/en-ww/).

## Data Storage

All data used by MadTunes remains on the user's device. The developer does not operate any servers that receive or store user data.

Users maintain full control over their music files and application data.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.
